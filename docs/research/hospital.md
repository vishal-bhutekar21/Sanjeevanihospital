## 2. Hospital Mission & Clinical Focus

Sanjeevani Multispeciality Hospital was founded in 2016 with the explicit purpose of bringing modern, specialized healthcare, critical care, and advanced surgical interventions to the Marathwada region, particularly Jalna and surrounding rural talukas.

### Core Pillars
1. **Critical Care & Emergency Resuscitation**: Round-the-clock intensive care unit (ICU) supported by intensivists and advanced life support.
2. **Advanced Surgical Care**: Minimally invasive (laparoscopic), orthopedic trauma/joint replacement, pediatric surgery, and urological interventions.
3. **Maternal & Child Health**: Comprehensive Obstetrics, Gynecology, and Neonatal / Pediatric care.
4. **Community Accessibility**: Active participation in government healthcare initiatives (Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana - MJPJAY) and corporate/cashless insurance desks.

---

## 3. Physical Infrastructure & Key Facilities

- **Intensive Care Units (ICU)**: Multi-bed critical care monitoring units equipped with multipara monitors, ventilators, defibrillators, and central oxygen.
- **Modular Operation Theatres**: Equipped with laminar air flow, C-arm imaging systems, and laparoscopic surgical towers.
- **Diagnostics**: In-house digital X-Ray, ultrasonography, 24x7 pathology laboratory, and ECG.
- **Dialysis Center**: Specialized hemodialysis machines for renal failure patients.
- **24x7 In-House Pharmacy**: Essential emergency medicines, surgical consumables, and chronic disease therapeutics.
- **Ambulance Unit**: Dedicated emergency patient transport (ALS/BLS).

## 4. Location Context & Accessibility

- Situated strategically at **Ambad Choufuli / Rishi Park** on the main Ambad Road in Jalna.
- Serves both Jalna urban residents and agrarian communities across Ambad, Partur, Badnapur, Bhokardan, and Mantha.
- Easy vehicular access with dedicated ambulance ramp and patient admission triage.
