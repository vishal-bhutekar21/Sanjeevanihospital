**Entity:** Sanjeevani Multispeciality Hospital  
**Scope:** Hospital Services, Critical Care Infrastructure, MJPJAY & Cashless Insurance Protocols

---

## 1. 24x7 Emergency & Critical Infrastructure

- **Emergency Triage & Resuscitation**: 24-hour emergency casualty unit staffed with emergency medical officers (EMOs) and nurses.
- **Intensive Care Unit (ICU)**:
  - Advanced multiparameter monitoring (Invasive/Non-invasive blood pressure, SpO2, EtCO2, ECG).
  - High-end mechanical ventilators with invasive and non-invasive modes (BiPAP/CPAP).
  - Syringe and infusion pumps for precision inotropic support.
  - Dedicated isolation bed for airborne/droplet infectious patients.
- **Operation Theatres (Modular OTs)**:
  - HEPA filtered laminar air flow preventing surgical site infections.
  - High-definition HD Laparoscopic camera towers.
  - C-Arm Fluoroscopy for intra-operative orthopedic fixation.

## 4. Government Health Schemes (MJPJAY / PM-JAY)

### Overview
Sanjeevani Multispeciality Hospital participates in state healthcare benefit initiatives, notably the **Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY)** and **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)**.

### Mandatory Compliance Disclaimer
> *"Eligibility, scheme coverage, document verification, and treatment package approvals are governed strictly by state scheme guidelines and Arogya Mitra pre-authorization. Hospital admission under scheme is finalized post verification."*

### Required Documents Checklist
1. Valid Ration Card (Yellow / Orange / Antyodaya Anna Yojana).
2. Ayushman Card / MJPJAY Health Card.
3. Patient Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card / Voter ID).
4. Relevant clinical diagnostic reports & doctor prescription.

---

## 5. Cashless Mediclaim & Corporate TPA Desk

- **Dedicated Insurance Desk**: Operating 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM for pre-authorization and claims processing.
- **Standard Process**:
  1. Patient presents TPA Health Card and Photo ID at admission.
  2. TPA Helpdesk sends pre-authorization request with estimated cost.
  3. Insurer issues Initial Approval Letter.
  4. Final discharge summary and itemized billing submitted for final settlement.
