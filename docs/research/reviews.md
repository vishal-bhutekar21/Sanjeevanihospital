# Research Dossier: Public Patient Reviews & Feedback Sentiment Analysis

**Entity:** Sanjeevani Multispeciality Hospital  
**Data Sources:** Public Google Business Profile Reviews, Patient Testimonials  
**Policy Standard:** Strict healthcare compliance — No fake reviews, transparent sentiment handling, and hospital administrative review mechanism.

---

## 1. Sentiment Categorization & Core Themes

Public feedback across search portals displays both strong positive experiences with treating clinicians and constructive feedback regarding administrative processes.

### Positive Themes (High Patient Satisfaction)
1. **Clinical Competence & Doctor Empathy**: High praise for senior doctors (Dr. Nishant Goyal for orthopedic surgeries, Dr. Shivdas Mirkad for pediatric care, Dr. Baliram Bagal for critical care recovery).
2. **Supportive Nursing in ICU**: Patients and attendants frequently noted attentive nursing staff during post-operative recovery.
3. **MJPJAY & Scheme Access**: Relief reported by families from rural Jalna who accessed life-saving treatments under government health schemes.
4. **Emergency Responsiveness**: Swift triage for fracture, trauma, and acute pediatric distress cases.

### Constructive / Operational Pain Points (Negative & Neutral Themes)
1. **Peak-Hour Waiting Times**: Crowding during morning OPD hours (11:00 AM – 01:00 PM) resulting in delayed consultation timing. *(Addressed by our new dynamic slot reservation system!)*
2. **Insurance Desk Documentation Delay**: Pre-authorization processing times during discharge bottlenecks.
3. **Cleanliness & Washroom Maintenance**: Occasional feedback regarding ward sanitation during heavy patient inflow days.
4. **Billing Clarifications**: Requests for clearer upfront itemization of consultation and diagnostic charges.

---

## 2. Platform Review & Testimonial Policy

To maintain the highest level of medical integrity:
1. **No Scraped Mass Publishing**: Do not inject unvetted scraped reviews directly into the production database.
2. **Representative Testimonials**: Display verified patient testimonials with explicit patient consent, date, and treatment department.
3. **Moderation Queue in Admin**: Hospital admin dashboard includes an approval workflow (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`) for submitted patient feedback.
4. **External Link**: Direct link to the hospital's official Google Maps listing allowing patients to view external unmoderated reviews.
